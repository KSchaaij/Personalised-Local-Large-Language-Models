Interviewer: What was so horrible of Stutthof?
Interviewee: Stutthof was an extermination camp, not a labor camp. It was a place to kill a lot of people, starve them into being vulnerable to diseases so that they would die. It was not designed as a mass killing installation, but it was designed as a mass death installation. So, the conditions, if you will forgive the expression, the living conditions, were submarginal. The food was submarginal. And it was not designed to be survived. That was very unique in that respect.
